Module 2

Precision & Conciseness

Master the Past Simple vs. Present Perfect to report completed work, and practice concise delivery.

Grammar: Past vs. Present Perfect

Past Simple (Finished action):

"I fixed the bug in the login module yesterday."

Present Perfect (Action relevant NOW):

"I have updated the documentation." (Meaning: it is ready for you to read now).

Pro-Tip: Never use "Yesterday I have done...". It is always "Yesterday I did...".

Module 4

Managing "Offline Flow"

Master strategies for handling interruptions and parking deep technical discussions for after the stand-up.

The "Parking Lot"

Use these phrases to pause a conversation that is taking too long.

  • 📌 "Let's take this offline."
  • 📌 "Can we sync up after the meeting?"
  • 📌 "Let's put a pin in that for now."
  • 📌 "We're getting a bit into the weeds."
