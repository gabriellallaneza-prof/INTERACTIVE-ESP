1. Enumeradoras (Chronological Order)

Orden

Indicate chronological progression, list of speakers, or logical argument order.

first, firstly, second, to begin with, next, finally, last(ly), then, subsequently, eventually, in the end, etc.

2. Adición (Addition)

Sumar

and, again, moreover, also, in addition, equally, similarly, likewise, etc.

3. Exclusión (Exclusion)

Excluir

Isolate elements, exceptional rules, or omit specific arguments.

but for, except for, without, lacking, etc.

4. Espaciales (Spatial / Location)

Espacio

Indicate location, environment, proximity, coordinates or physical direction.

there, here, further away, down there, beyond, in front of, etc.

5. Temporales (Temporal / Time)

Tiempo

Specify exact timing, concurrency of events, or future prospects.

now, then, meanwhile, in the future, soon, early, late, lately, when, etc.

6. Contraste o Adversativas (Adversative Contrast)

Contraste

Signal dynamic opposition, reservations, counter-arguments, or alternatives.

but, yet, still, however, only, even so, nevertheless, instead, by way of contrast, rather, whereas, while, when, conversely, etc.

7. Contraste o Concesión (Concession)

Concesión

Recognize the validity of a prior fact, yet maintain that the succeeding fact remains true nonetheless.

although, though, however, even if/though, except that, no matter (how), in spite of, despite, notwithstanding, pronouns/adverbs ending in "-ever" (however).

8. Condición (Condition)

Condición

Establish constraints, requirements, and state hypothetical situations.

if, unless, in case that, provided that, as if, though, whether...or not, as long as, if only, in case of, but for.

9. Propósito (Purpose / Intent)

Propósito

Define the intended goal, design target, or desired system outcome.

that, in order that, so that, in case, in order to.

10. Consecuencia (Result / Consequence)

Efecto

Illustrate downstream fallout, direct logical products, or experimental conclusions.

therefore, thus, hence, so, that is why, owing to this, for that reason, consequently, then, to such an extent that..., in that case.

11. Causa (Cause / Origin)

Causa

Explain root incidents, failures, errors, or source triggers.

for, for that reason, because, since, as, now that..., because of.

12. Comparativo (Comparative Comparison)

Comparar

Establish parity, similar operating loops, or equivalent architectural paradigms.

similarly, like, the same as, in the same way.

13. Explicativo (Explanatory)

Explicar

Rephrase concepts, provide operational clarity, or clarify code specifications.

namely, thus, in other words, that is, by this we mean.

14. Ejemplificador (Exemplifying)
